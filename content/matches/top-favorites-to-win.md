Brazil lead the World Cup 2026 outright betting at most bookmakers, typically priced around 5/1. France follow closely at 6/1, with Argentina defending their 2022 title at 7/1.

England at 8/1 represent the standout value pick given their squad depth and favorable draw potential. Germany, playing on home continent soil with strong European support, are worth considering at 10/1.

Dark horses include Portugal (without Ronaldo for the first time in decades), Spain, and surprisingly Belgium who have rebuilt quietly after their golden generation retired.
